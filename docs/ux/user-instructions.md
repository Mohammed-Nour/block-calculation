# BlockCalc in one page

BlockCalc works out the materials and cost for a block order, when it will be ready, and what has been produced and collected.

## 1. Check Settings once

Open **Settings**.

- Enter the price per ton of sand, 13 mm stone and cement.
- Weigh one full wheelbarrow of sand and of stone, and enter the kilograms.
- Enter how many full-block and half-block molds you have.
- For each mix, enter how many full blocks and how many half blocks one batch makes.

Press **Save settings**. The box at the top tells you if anything is still missing.

## 2. Create a project and save its cost

Open **New project**.

1. Type a name and the date production should start.
2. Enter the number of full blocks (400 × 200 × 200 mm) and half blocks (200 × 200 × 200 mm).
3. Choose a mix.
4. Press **Calculate cost** and check the materials and total.
5. Press **Save project and estimate**.

The saved cost keeps today's prices, even if you change Settings later.

## 3. Plan and track production

On the project page:

- **Completion forecast** shows when the order should be ready.
- **Plan production** splits the order into runs that fit your molds.
- When blocks go into the molds, press **Start** on that run.
- After at least 48 hours, check the blocks, then press **Confirm ready**.

## 4. Record collection

When the customer takes blocks away, enter the numbers under **Collection** and press **Record collection**. You cannot collect more than is ready.

## Finding projects

**Projects** lists every order. Use the status buttons (Draft, Planned, In production…) to filter the list.
